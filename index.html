FAQ

Common
Questions

Everything you need to know about FinTracker and our pricing plans.

What's the difference between Free and Premium plans?

Free plan includes basic features like expense tracking, simple dashboard, and monthly reports with 1 family group. Premium plans unlock advanced charts, annual reports, unlimited groups, CSV exports, priority support, and advanced recurring expense management.

Is the Lifetime plan really forever?

Yes! The Lifetime plan is a one-time payment of €100 that gives you permanent access to all premium features, including all future updates and new features at no additional cost. No recurring payments, no hidden fees.

Can I switch between plans?

Absolutely! You can upgrade from Free to Premium or Annual at any time. If you're on Monthly Premium, you can switch to Annual and we'll credit your remaining balance. You can also upgrade to Lifetime at a discounted rate based on your current subscription.

How does family sharing work?

Free users get 1 family group. Premium users get unlimited groups. You can invite family members or roommates via email to share expense tracking. Each member can add expenses, view shared dashboards, and collaborate on budgets while maintaining privacy for personal expenses.

Is my financial data secure?

Yes! We use Firebase for secure cloud storage with bank-level encryption. Your data is encrypted both in transit and at rest. We never share or sell your information. Premium users get priority backup for extra peace of mind.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and Apple Pay. All payments are processed securely through Stripe. Prices are shown in Euros but we support multiple currencies.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, you can cancel anytime with no questions asked. Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled before the next billing cycle. Annual subscriptions are non-refundable but you keep access until the end of your billing period. We also offer a 7-day money-back guarantee on all plans.

Do you offer student or family discounts?

Yes! Students with valid .edu email get 20% off annual plans. Families (3+ members sharing an account) get 15% off. Contact our support team with proof of student status or family details to claim your discount.
