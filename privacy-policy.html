Privacy Policy

FinTracker respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your data.

Information we collect

  • Account data: name and email address (Google Sign-In) and a user identifier (UID).
  • User content: data you enter in the app (expenses, categories, values, dates, notes).
  • Basic technical data: information needed to operate authentication and app sessions.

How we use information

  • Authenticate users and keep accounts secure.
  • Store and display your financial records.
  • Provide and improve app functionality.

Data storage and processors

We use Google Firebase services to run core features:

  • Firebase Authentication — sign-in and identity.
  • Cloud Firestore — storage of expense data linked to your account.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your personal data with third parties, except as required to provide the service (Firebase) or when legally required.

Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. Access is restricted via authentication and database security rules.

Data retention

Your data is kept while your account is active. You can request deletion at any time.

Data deletion request

To request deletion of your account and associated data, email us at fintracker.apps@gmail.com with subject: Data Deletion Request.
