Privacy Policy — FinTracker

FinTracker respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your data.

Information we collect

  • Account data: name and email address (when you sign in with Google), and a user identifier (UID).
  • User content: financial data you enter in the app (e.g., expenses, categories, values, dates, notes).
  • Basic app data: technical information necessary to operate the app (e.g., authentication tokens and session-related data).

How we use information

  • Authenticate users and keep accounts secure.
  • Store and display your financial records.
  • Provide and improve app functionality.

Data storage and processors

We use Google Firebase services to run core features:

  • Firebase Authentication — for sign-in and user identity.
  • Cloud Firestore — to store your expense data linked to your account.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your personal data with third parties, except as required to provide the service (e.g., Firebase as described above) or when legally required.

Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. Data is stored using Google Firebase infrastructure and access is restricted through authentication and database security rules.

Data retention

Your data is kept while your account remains active. You can request deletion at any time.

Your rights and choices

  • You may request access to or deletion of your data.
  • You may stop using the app at any time.

Data deletion request

To request deletion of your account and associated data, email us at fintracker.app@gmail.com using the subject: Data Deletion Request.

Contact
